# Daily motivation quote sources

The home screen mixes original PRIME powerlifting-minded prompts with these short, attributed excerpts:

- David Goggins: “We're either getting better or we're getting worse.” [Audible, quotes from David Goggins](https://www.audible.co.uk/blog/quotes-david-goggins)
- Muhammad Ali: “The will must be stronger than the skill.” [ESPN, Muhammad Ali's 10 best quotes](https://www.espn.com/boxing/story/_/id/15930888/muhammad-ali-10-best-quotes)
- LeBron James: “You've got to find a way to bounce back.” [The Talks, LeBron James interview](https://the-talks.com/interview/lebron-james/)
- Virat Kohli: “When you become fit, you feel you can do anything.” [ESPNcricinfo, Have never been as fit as I am today - Kohli](https://www.cricinfo.com/story/have-never-been-as-fit-as-i-am-today-says-india-test-captain-virat-kohli-1030559)

The widely circulated “Impossible is just a big word…” text is deliberately not attributed to Ali: Quote Investigator traces it to an Adidas campaign manifesto by copywriter Aimee Lehto, with “Impossible is nothing” credited to Boyd Coyner: https://quoteinvestigator.com/2017/11/28/impossible-is/
